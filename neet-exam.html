03 · MARKING SCHEME

Every Answer Changes Your Score

NEET uses positive marks for a correct answer and negative marking for an incorrect answer.

CORRECT+4Four marks
WRONG−1One mark deducted
UNANSWERED0No mark
Basic score formula(Correct × 4) − Wrong
IMPORTANT OFFICIAL CASES

What If a Question Has a Problem?

More than one option is correct

+4 is awarded to candidates who marked any of the officially correct options.

All options are correct

+4 is awarded to candidates who attempted the question.

Question is wrong / dropped

+4 is awarded to all candidates who appeared, whether attempted or not.

04 · MODE, OMR & LANGUAGE

What “Pen & Paper” Means for You

The question booklet is physical and answers are recorded on an OMR answer sheet. Exam-day OMR instructions must be followed exactly as issued by NTA.

13 question-paper media

07 · COMMON CONFUSIONS

Questions Aspirants Commonly Ask

Short answers first. Detailed exam-day instructions should always follow the latest official bulletin and admit-card instructions.

Are all 180 questions compulsory?

Yes. The 2026 official pattern states that the paper has 180 compulsory questions to be attempted in 180 minutes.

Is there negative marking?

Yes. A correct answer gets +4, an incorrect marked answer gets −1, and an unanswered question gets 0.

Is NEET online or offline?

NEET (UG) 2026 is notified in pen-and-paper mode, using a physical test booklet and OMR response sheet.

Is Biology really half of the paper?

Yes. Biology has 90 questions carrying 360 marks out of the total 720 marks.

Do I have to follow a fixed subject order?

The official pattern section defines subjects, questions, marks and duration; it does not prescribe an Academy time split. Build your attempt order through mock-test practice while following all booklet/OMR instructions.

Can I write the paper in Telugu?

Yes. Telugu is one of the 13 listed media for NEET (UG) 2026, with availability subject to the official examination-centre language rules.
