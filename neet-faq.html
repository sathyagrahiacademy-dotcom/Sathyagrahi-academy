NEET BASICS · OFFICIALWhat is NEET (UG)?
+

NEET (UG) is the National Eligibility cum Entrance Test used as the common entrance examination for undergraduate medical education and other courses/applications notified under the applicable laws and official bulletin. NTA conducts NEET (UG).

Read About NEET →
02
NEET BASICS · OFFICIALWho conducts NEET?
+

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts NEET (UG). Counselling and admission are handled separately by the competent counselling/admission authorities.

See Official Source Map →
03
NEET BASICS · OFFICIALIs NEET only for MBBS?
+

No. The current official NEET framework also applies to other notified undergraduate medical/AYUSH-related admissions. The exact use of a NEET score should always be checked in the current official bulletin and the relevant admission authority’s rules.

04
ELIGIBILITY · OFFICIALHow do I know whether I am eligible for NEET?
+

Eligibility depends on the current NEET Information Bulletin, including qualifying-examination, subject, age and other applicable conditions. Do not rely on an old-year eligibility screenshot. Check the current bulletin before applying.

Open Official Sources →
05
EXAM PATTERN · OFFICIAL 2026How many questions and marks are in the current NEET pattern?
+

For NEET (UG) 2026, the official pattern has 180 compulsory MCQs for 720 marks: 45 Physics, 45 Chemistry and 90 Biology questions.

Explore Exam Pattern →
06
MARKING · OFFICIAL 2026Is there negative marking in NEET?
+

Yes, under the 2026 official scheme: a correct answer gets +4, an incorrect marked answer gets −1 and an unanswered question gets 0. Special treatment can apply to officially dropped/incorrect questions as described in the bulletin.

07
EXAM MODE · OFFICIAL 2026Is NEET online or pen-and-paper?
+

NEET (UG) 2026 is conducted in pen-and-paper mode. Aspirants record answers on an OMR response sheet according to the exam instructions.

08
LANGUAGE · OFFICIAL 2026Can I write NEET in Telugu?
+

Yes. Telugu is one of the question-paper media listed for NEET (UG) 2026. Candidates should check the current bulletin for language and examination-centre rules.

09
EXAM STRATEGY · ACADEMY GUIDANCEWhich subject should I attempt first?
+

There is no single best order for every aspirant. Use full-length mock tests to find the order that gives you the best combination of accuracy, confidence and time control. Do not choose an order only because someone else follows it.

10
SYLLABUS · OFFICIALWhere should I check the complete NEET syllabus?
+

Use the current official syllabus published through the competent official source. Sathyagrahi Academy’s Syllabus Explorer organises that official scope into units and topics for easier understanding.

Open Syllabus Explorer →
11
SYLLABUS USE · ACADEMY GUIDANCEShould I study the syllabus in the same order it is printed?
+

Not necessarily. A syllabus tells you what can be tested; it is not automatically the best learning sequence. Preparation order should consider foundations, chapter connections, current level, practice requirements and revision needs.

Use Preparation Guide →
12
NCERT · ACADEMY GUIDANCEIs NCERT enough for NEET?
+

NCERT is a core learning source, especially for Biology and many Chemistry areas, but preparation is not just reading. Physics and Physical Chemistry require strong concept application and numerical practice; every subject also needs PYQs, revision and testing.

13
SYLLABUS CHANGES · OFFICIALCan the NEET syllabus change?
+

Yes, official syllabus publications can be updated. Always identify the exam year on the syllabus and verify it from NMC/NTA official sources rather than using an undated PDF.

14
STARTING PREPARATION · ACADEMY GUIDANCEI am new to NEET. What should I do first?
+

First understand the exam pattern and official syllabus. Then identify your current level in Physics, Chemistry and Biology. Build concept clarity chapter by chapter, practise questions, revise repeatedly and use tests to decide what needs improvement.

Open Preparation Guide →
15
PHYSICS · ACADEMY GUIDANCEWhat if Physics is my weakest subject?
+

Do not begin by chasing difficult questions. Build the chapter’s basic language, quantities, units, graphs and core equations first. Then practise one concept at a time before mixing concepts. Weak Physics improves through structured repetition, not through memorising formulas alone.

16
CHEMISTRY · ACADEMY GUIDANCEShould I study all Chemistry in the same way?
+

No. Physical Chemistry needs concepts + calculations; Inorganic Chemistry needs precise NCERT understanding, trends and revision; Organic Chemistry needs mechanism/reaction understanding, connections and repeated practice.

17
BIOLOGY · ACADEMY GUIDANCEHow should I read NCERT Biology?
+

Read for exact meaning, not just speed. Pay attention to definitions, examples, exceptions, diagrams, tables and similar-looking terms. After reading, close the book and recall, then solve questions and return to the lines you misunderstood.

18
DAILY STUDY · ACADEMY GUIDANCEHow many hours should I study every day?
+

There is no universal number. Measure useful output instead: concepts completed, questions practised, revision done, mistakes corrected and tests analysed. A sustainable focused routine is more valuable than a large hour-count with low concentration.

19
REVISION · ACADEMY GUIDANCEWhat does proper revision mean?
+

Revision is not simply rereading everything. Recall what you know, review formulas/NCERT lines/short notes, solve selected questions, check old errors and retest weak areas. The aim is faster and more accurate retrieval.

20
MOCK TESTS · ACADEMY GUIDANCEWhen should I start full-length tests?
+

Begin chapter and part-syllabus tests early. Full-length tests become more valuable as syllabus coverage grows. Do not wait for “perfect preparation”; testing itself teaches time management, stamina, error patterns and revision priorities.

21
PYQs · ACADEMY GUIDANCEWhy are previous-year questions important?
+

PYQs show how syllabus concepts have been converted into actual questions. They help you recognise question style, recurring concepts, NCERT focus, calculation depth and common traps.

Open Previous Papers →
22
PYQs · ACADEMY GUIDANCEHow many years of PYQs should I solve?
+

Use a meaningful multi-year set rather than chasing a number. More recent papers help you understand the current style, while older relevant questions can strengthen concepts. Always account for syllabus changes before treating an old question as current-syllabus practice.

23
PYQ ANALYSIS · ACADEMY GUIDANCEIs solving the question enough?
+

No. After solving, identify the chapter, exact concept, why the correct option works, why you made an error, and whether the issue was knowledge, calculation, reading or time. That turns a PYQ into a learning tool.

24
QUESTION TYPES · ACADEMY GUIDANCEAre Physics, Chemistry and Biology questions tested in the same style?
+

No. Physics often emphasises concept application/numericals; Chemistry varies across Physical, Inorganic and Organic styles; Biology frequently demands precise NCERT reading, statements, diagrams, examples and distinctions. These are learning categories, not official NTA classifications.

25
RESULT · OFFICIALWhere should I download my NEET scorecard?
+

Use the official NEET/NTA portal and the current official result/scorecard service. Do not enter credentials into an unofficial result page.

See Official Sources →
26
RESULT TERMS · EXPLANATIONWhat is the difference between score, percentile and AIR?
+

Score is the marks obtained under the exam marking scheme. Percentile is a relative measure used in the result framework. AIR means All India Rank. Admission decisions then depend on the applicable counselling merit, category/quota and seat availability.

27
CUT-OFF · EXPLANATIONIs qualifying cut-off the same as medical-college admission cut-off?
+

No. Qualifying the examination makes you eligible under the applicable rules, but the rank/score needed for a particular seat can be much more competitive and varies by counselling route, category, college, quota, round and year.

28
COUNSELLING · OFFICIALDoes NTA allot medical seats?
+

No. NTA conducts NEET and publishes examination results. Medical-seat counselling/allotment is handled by the competent counselling authorities such as MCC and designated state authorities.

29
COUNSELLING · OFFICIALWhat is AIQ counselling?
+

All India Quota and other participating UG medical counselling categories handled by MCC are governed by the current MCC scheme. Registration, schedule, seat matrix and allotment should be checked directly on MCC.

Open Counselling Guide →
30
TELANGANA · OFFICIALWhat is “Convener Quota” in Telangana?
+

“Convener Quota” is a commonly used informal term. KNRUHS officially uses Competent Authority Quota. The current KNRUHS prospectus/notification should be used for eligibility, reservation, local-area and seat-allotment rules.

31
TELANGANA · OFFICIALAre Management B and NRI C the same?
+

No. They are different categories within the separately notified Management Quota process. C/NRI requires the notified NRI eligibility/document conditions. Current-year KNRUHS prospectus and fee/seat notifications take priority.

32
CHOICE FILLING · ACADEMY GUIDANCEShould I copy another student’s college preference list?
+

No. College preference depends on your own course preference, eligibility, fees, bond/service conditions, location, category/quota and willingness to join. Use historical closing ranks as supporting data, not as a substitute for your real preference.

33
PARENTS · ACADEMY GUIDANCEHow much pressure should parents put on a NEET aspirant?
+

Track routines and responsibilities without turning every conversation into a marks discussion. Ask what the student learned, where they are struggling and what support is needed. Consistency improves better in a stable environment than under constant comparison.

34
PARENTS · ACADEMY GUIDANCEWhat should we do if test marks suddenly fall?
+

First analyse the reason: incomplete syllabus, weak revision, careless errors, time pressure, difficult paper or stress. One test is data, not a final judgment. Correct the cause and compare performance across several tests.

35
PARENTS · ACADEMY GUIDANCEShould parents compare their child with toppers or friends?
+

Comparison can hide the actual learning problem. Compare the student with their own previous preparation: syllabus coverage, accuracy, revision quality, test behaviour and error reduction.

36
PARENTS · ACADEMY GUIDANCEHow can parents help without teaching the subjects?
+

Help protect study time, sleep, meals and a calm environment; track important official deadlines; encourage honest test analysis; and notice when the student needs academic or emotional support. You do not have to know Physics or Biology to support a good system.

37
PARENTS · ACADEMY GUIDANCEWhat if the student says “I studied a lot” but marks do not improve?
+

Shift the discussion from hours to evidence: Can the student recall the concept? Solve questions? Avoid repeated mistakes? Complete revision? Perform under test conditions? Study time becomes useful only when it changes performance.
