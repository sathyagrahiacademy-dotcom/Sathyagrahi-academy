WHY THIS PAGE EXISTS

One NEET Journey — Different Official Authorities

The same student may need NTA for the examination, NMC for syllabus/regulatory references, MCC for All India counselling, KNRUHS for Telangana state admissions and NCERT for official school textbooks. Knowing the role of each source prevents confusion.

03 · HOW TO VERIFY A NOTICE

Six Checks Before You Trust a PDF or Message

A notice can look professional and still be old, edited or unofficial. Use a simple verification habit.

01Check the Domain

Did the information come from the official authority’s website?

→
02Check the Year

Is it for your current admission/exam year?

→
03Check the Date

A newer notice may replace an older one.

→
04Check the Title

Make sure the notice is actually about your course/category/round.

→
05Look for Corrigenda

Correction notices can change dates or instructions.

→
06Act Only After Verification

Use the latest official instruction before payment, registration or reporting.
