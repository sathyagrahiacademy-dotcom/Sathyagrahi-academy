02 · KNOW YOUR COUNSELLING ROUTE

All India Counselling and State Counselling Are Different Paths

An aspirant may be eligible to participate in more than one counselling system. Registration, schedule, rules and seat pools are not automatically the same.

AIQ
ALL INDIA / MCC ROUTE

Medical Counselling Committee

MCC conducts counselling for 15% All India Quota seats in participating government medical/dental colleges and also for specified participating Central/Deemed/AIIMS/JIPMER and other categories under the current scheme.

  • Separate MCC registration
  • National-level choice filling
  • MCC seat matrix and schedule
  • Four AIQ rounds in the 2026 scheme

Your State Admission Authority

03 · TELANGANA QUOTA TYPES — UNDERSTAND THE NAMES

Convener, Management and NRI — What Do They Actually Mean?

These names refer to different admission seat categories and counselling routes. The exact seat matrix, fee, eligibility and document rules must always be checked in the current KNRUHS prospectus/notification.

First clarification: “Convener Quota” is a commonly used student/parent term. KNRUHS officially uses the term Competent Authority Quota.
A
COMMONLY CALLED “CONVENER QUOTA”

Competent Authority Quota

These seats are filled through the state competent authority counselling process according to the applicable state eligibility, merit, reservation, local-area/category and seat-matrix rules.

  • Official Telangana authority: KNRUHS
  • NEET qualification is required
  • State merit / applicable reservation rules matter
  • Web options and allotment happen through the notified counselling process
THINKState-authority counselling seat
B
PRIVATE COLLEGE MANAGEMENT SEAT CATEGORY

Management Quota — B Category

Management B-category seats in affiliated private medical/dental colleges are handled through the separately notified Management Quota admission process. They are not the same as Competent Authority seats.

  • Separate management-quota registration/prospectus may apply
  • NEET qualification and notified eligibility are required
  • Candidates exercise web options under the notified process
  • Allotment follows the applicable merit, eligibility and seat availability rules
THINKManagement seat through official counselling
C
MANAGEMENT QUOTA · NRI CATEGORY

NRI Quota — C / NRI Category

This is the NRI-category route within the separately notified Management Quota process. A candidate must satisfy the NRI-related eligibility and documentary requirements prescribed for that admission year.

  • NEET qualification remains essential
  • NRI / sponsor / relationship documents may be required as notified
  • Separate C/NRI eligibility conditions apply
  • Do not assume that paying a higher fee alone makes a candidate eligible for NRI quota
THINKNRI-eligible management-category seat

04 · AIQ COUNSELLING ROUNDS · 2026 REFERENCE

Four Rounds in the Current MCC AIQ Scheme

The 2026 MCC bulletin describes AIQ Round 1, Round 2, Round 3 and Stray Vacancy Round. Upgradation is available up to Round 3, subject to the scheme.

ROUND 1Start the Process

Registration, choice filling, allotment and the applicable reporting/upgradation decision.

ROUND 2Another Opportunity

Eligible candidates can participate according to MCC’s Round 2 rules and schedule.

ROUND 3Commit Carefully

Round 3 is more restrictive. Under the current scheme, candidates cannot resign/exit an allotted Round 3 seat.

STRAY VACANCYFinal Vacant Seats

Conducted for remaining eligible vacancies under the official stray-vacancy rules.

Rules can change by year.

Use this section to understand the structure, but use the latest MCC bulletin and schedule before making an actual counselling decision.

05 · IMPORTANT 2026 COUNSELLING FLOW

Freeze vs Float — Understand Your Intention

The 2026 MCC scheme uses these two pathways for allotted candidates depending on whether they want to retain the seat or remain open to upgradation.

FREEZE

I Am Satisfied With This Seat

You do not want upgradation to the next round. Under the 2026 MCC scheme, a Freeze candidate who wants to join proceeds to the allotted institute for physical joining and document verification.

Think: RETAIN + JOIN
FLOAT

I Want to Keep This Seat but Try to Upgrade

You give willingness for upgradation. Under the 2026 MCC scheme, required documents are uploaded for online verification and physical reporting is not required at that stage for Float candidates.

Think: HOLD + UPGRADE
ASK YOURSELF

“If I get no better seat in the next round, am I comfortable continuing with this allotment?”

This is not a substitute for the official rules; it is a simple way to understand the decision mindset before reading the detailed scheme.

06 · CHOICE FILLING GUIDE

Your Preference Order Matters

Choice filling should reflect what you genuinely prefer — not a random list copied from someone else.

1Build a College List

Start from all colleges you are actually willing and eligible to join.

2Verify Before Ranking

Check fees, bond/service conditions, location, category/quota eligibility and official college information.

3Order by True Preference

Put the seat you want most above seats you want less. Do not rank by guesswork alone.

4Review Before Locking

Once choices are locked under the official process, changes may not be permitted for that window.

08 · COMMON COUNSELLING MISTAKES

Small Errors Can Become Big Admission Problems

Most counselling mistakes are preventable when the aspirant checks the official source, deadlines and documents carefully.

01Missing a Deadline

Registration, choice locking, reporting and resignation windows are time-bound.

02Wrong Choice Order

Do not place a less-preferred college above a college you truly want more.

03Not Checking Fees / Bond

Verify institutional fee, service/bond conditions and other obligations before filling choices.

04Assuming Eligibility

Check domicile, category, quota and institutional eligibility before selecting seats.

05Ignoring Document Mismatch

Name/category/certificate inconsistencies can affect verification.

06Depending on Forwarded Messages

Use WhatsApp/social media only as alerts; verify final decisions on official portals.

09 · COUNSELLING TERMS YOU MUST KNOW

Understand the Language Before You Make Decisions

Open each term for a simple explanation.

AIQ All India Quota

The national counselling seat pool handled by MCC for eligible participating seats under the official scheme.

State Quota State Counselling

Seats handled under the rules of the designated state counselling authority.

Choice Filling Your preference list

The process of selecting and arranging colleges/courses in the order you prefer.

Seat Matrix Available seats

The official list of seats/categories available for a counselling round.

Freeze No upgradation

In the 2026 MCC scheme, a candidate satisfied with the allotted seat who does not seek upgradation.

Float Willing to upgrade

In the 2026 MCC scheme, an allotted candidate who remains willing to move to a higher-preference seat in the next applicable round.

Stray Vacancy Final vacant-seat round

A later counselling round for eligible remaining vacancies under the applicable official rules.

Reporting Complete admission formalities

The required joining/document-verification/admission process after allotment, according to the applicable round and scheme.

Competent Authority Quota Commonly called Convener Quota

The official Telangana state-authority counselling route. “Convener quota” is a commonly used informal name; always follow the KNRUHS Competent Authority notification.

Management B Category Management Quota

A separately notified management-seat category in applicable private affiliated colleges, filled through the official Management Quota admission process.

C / NRI Category NRI Quota

The NRI-category route under the Management Quota process, subject to the academic year’s notified NRI eligibility and documentary requirements.
