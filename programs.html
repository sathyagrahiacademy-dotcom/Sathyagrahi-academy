SATHYAGRAHI ACADEMY PROGRAMS

A Complete NEET Preparation Journey

Our programs are designed to guide students through every important stage of preparation — understanding concepts, building consistent study habits, practising questions, revising at the right time, writing examinations and using performance data to decide what to do next.

01

NEET Learning & Preparation Program

A structured learning journey for students who need clear direction in Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

  • Official syllabus-based learning progress
  • Subject and topic-wise preparation tracking
  • Daily study logging and focused preparation
  • Concept learning, practice and completion tracking
Best for building a clear and disciplined preparation routine.
02

Revision & Retention Program

Learning becomes useful only when students can remember and recall it at the right time.

  • Revision due tracking for completed topics
  • Planned revision cycle after learning
  • Revision history linked to each syllabus topic
  • Preparation guidance based on pending revision
Designed to protect learning from being forgotten.
03

Online Examination & Assessment Program

Students test their understanding through structured online examinations and receive clear performance feedback.

  • Secure exam access through Exam Code and Password
  • NTA-style online examination experience
  • Published results and downloadable scorecards
  • Correct, wrong and unattempted answer analysis
Assessment is used to guide the next preparation step.
04

Performance & Progress Guidance

Student preparation is not judged by one mark alone. Learning, revision and exam performance are viewed together.

  • Physics, Chemistry and Biology progress
  • Syllabus completion and study-time tracking
  • Exam average, accuracy and recent performance
  • Preparation history and next-action guidance
Helps students understand where they are and what they should improve next.
05

Study Material & Academy Support

Students receive learning resources and important Academy information directly through the Student Portal.

  • Subject-wise study material access
  • Academy notifications and important updates
  • Help & Feedback support system
  • Student profile and attendance access
Everything important stays organised in one student portal.
06

Personal Preparation Direction

The Academy's role is to help students make better preparation decisions with clarity and consistency.

  • Today’s next-step guidance
  • Learning and revision priority visibility
  • Preparation tasks assigned when required
  • Progress understood through each student’s own journey
