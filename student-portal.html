01 · BEGIN HERE

NEET in 60 Seconds

First understand the big picture. Detailed rules and preparation come later.

60SEC
01

What is NEET?

National Eligibility cum Entrance Test (UG) is the common national entrance examination used for admission to undergraduate medical education covered by the applicable laws and official notifications.

02

Who conducts it?

The National Testing Agency (NTA) conducts NEET (UG). NTA has been conducting the examination since 2019.

03

Who should understand it?

Students planning a medical or other NEET-linked undergraduate pathway should understand the exam early — ideally from Intermediate / Class 11 itself.

04

Where can it lead?

NEET scores are used for MBBS and other notified undergraduate medical/AYUSH pathways. The exact course coverage must always follow the current official bulletin.

Think of NEET as a journey, not just a three-hour exam:Learn → Practise → Revise → Test → Improve → Qualify → Counselling → Admission
02 · THE STORY

How NEET Evolved

A short history so you understand why a single national medical entrance exists today.

BEFORE

Multiple Entrance Routes

Medical admission involved different national, state and institutional entrance routes. Students often had to follow more than one system.

2013

NEET-UG Era Begins

Official MCI/NMC records show NEET-UG as the common entrance test for MBBS admission from 2013, followed by major legal developments.

2016

NEET Returns as the Common Route

Supreme Court orders and subsequent legal changes restored the common NEET framework for undergraduate medical admissions.

2019 →

NTA Conducts NEET

NTA has conducted NEET (UG) since 2019. The present system continues as a common and uniform national entrance framework.

History reference: official NMC NEET-UG records and NTA's official NEET information pages.

03 · KNOW THE SYSTEM

Who Runs the NEET Journey?

One common confusion is thinking one organisation does everything. It does not. Different authorities have different roles.

NTA

Conducts the Examination

Application process, test administration, answer-key process, result and All India Rank responsibilities fall within NTA's examination role.

→
NMC

Medical Education Framework

The National Medical Commission is the statutory medical regulator. The NEET framework for undergraduate medical courses is rooted in the applicable medical education law and regulations.

→
MCC

Central Counselling Role

The Medical Counselling Committee conducts counselling for the seat categories and institutions placed under its official UG counselling mandate.

→
STATE

State Counselling

State counselling authorities handle the state-side counselling process according to applicable state rules and the current counselling schedule.

Simple way to rememberNTA = Exam · NMC = Medical education framework · MCC/State Authorities = Counselling & seat allocation roles

07 · REMOVE THE FEAR

NEET Myths vs Reality

Many students become afraid of NEET before they even understand it. Replace assumptions with a practical view.

MYTH

“Only toppers can prepare for NEET.”

REALITY

Consistent learning, revision, question practice and error correction matter more than a label like “topper”.

MYTH

“I must be very strong in Maths for Physics.”

REALITY

NEET Physics needs specific mathematical tools and regular numerical practice. Those tools can be learned step by step.

MYTH

“I should finish everything before taking tests.”

REALITY

Small topic and chapter tests should be part of learning itself. Testing helps you discover what needs improvement.

MYTH

“More study hours always mean better preparation.”

REALITY

Focused study, active recall, practice, revision and analysis are more useful than simply counting hours.

08 · LEARN THE LANGUAGE OF NEET

Important Terms You Will Keep Hearing

Click a term to understand it in simple language.

AIR All India Rank

Your position in the all-India ranking generated from the NEET result, subject to the official result methodology.

Cut-off Qualifying threshold

The officially declared qualifying threshold. Admission cut-offs can be different from the qualifying cut-off and depend on counselling, category, college and seat availability.

AIQ All India Quota

A counselling/seat category used in the national UG counselling system. Exact coverage is explained in the current MCC bulletin.

State Quota State counselling seats

Seats handled through the relevant state counselling process, subject to domicile and other applicable state rules.

Choice Filling Preference order

The counselling stage where eligible candidates enter and order their preferred colleges/courses according to the applicable process.

Seat Allotment Counselling result

The process through which a seat may be allotted based on rank, category, choices, availability and counselling rules.
